## AI Policy:
<span style="color: #c7ced3;">
AI can be used as a tool much like stack exchange. You may ask: </span>

>  <span style="color: #5cad9a;">"how do you construct a scatter plot in python"</span> or <span style="color: #5cad9a;">"why is my code outputting this error"</span> 
<span style="color: #c7ced3;">

You should not ask it prompt specific questions such as: </span>

> <span style="color: #5cad9a;">"interpret this data"</span> or <span style="color: #5cad9a;">"write a solver that does X"</span> 
<span style="color: #c7ced3;">
as you construct the project. Your team should be coming up with the core ideas and implementation yourselves. We plan to have a live presentation followed by very detailed questions for finalist teams so **it will be clear to us if you have used AI in place of truly understanding the modeling problem.** </span>
