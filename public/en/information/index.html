Information

Theme:

Our theme for this year is Raiders of the Lost Model. In this high stakes adventure, Dr. Jones must find the lost model before the forces of evil use it to destroy humanity! She does not know what the model entails and only has 12 hours so you must help explore them with her and uncover it first. Her only clue is a few details left behind by another explorer (your prompt).

Prompts:

Choose one prompt to submit. Prompts will be released at 10:00am the day of the competition.

Schedule:

The modeling competition will take place 9:00 am October 3rd, 2026 in George Tower Room TS3 0270.

Day 1

9:00am: Opening Remarks

9:15am: Keynote Speaker (TBA)

9:45am: Instructions

10:00am: Competition Starts

10:00pm: Competition Ends, All Materials Due

Day 2

9:00am: Finalists Announced

1:00pm: 5 Finalists Groups Return to George Tower Room TS3 0270 to Give 7 Minute Presentation of Projects (3 minutes of questions).

3:00pm: Winners Announced

Rules:

1. Teams are 1-4 members

2. Submit your report and github repository to this form by: October 3rd at 10pm. You can submit multiple times.

3. You may use AI but you must specify how. For example, you should comment your code indicating which sections are AI, and you must explain its contributions in the report. You may not use AI to come up with your idea and if it is deemed more than supplimental, you will be disqualified (see AI Policy for more details).

4. Your report must contain the names of all your team members. You must write this yourself without AI.

AI Policy:

AI can be used as a tool much like stack exchange. You may ask:

“how do you construct a scatter plot in python” or “why is my code outputting this error”

You should not ask it prompt specific questions such as:

“interpret this data” or “write a solver that does X” as you construct the project. Your team should be coming up with the core ideas and implementation yourselves. We plan to have a live presentation followed by very detailed questions for finalist teams so it will be clear to us if you have used AI in place of truly understanding the modeling problem.

Finalists will be asked detailed questions during their presentation, so we will know if you used AI more than what is acceptable.

Submission:

Submit your project to the link here. You may submit as many times as you need. We will evaluate your most recent submission. All report submissions need to be in the format: groupname_2026model_submission.pdf. Submit a link to your repository along with the report.

You must submit your autograder report with your submission.
